Record a decision in `state/DECISIONS.md`.

A decision belongs here when it shapes the repository and a future reader would otherwise ask
"why is it like this?" — an architecture choice, a convention, a deliberate omission, a trade-off.

Append a new entry in this shape:

```markdown
## D-0NN — <short title>
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD · **By:** <who decided>

<What was decided, in one or two sentences. Plain and specific.>

*Why:* <The reasoning. What alternative lost, and what it cost.>
```

Rules:

- **Append only.** Never edit or delete an existing decision. If a decision is reversed, write a
  new one that says so and references the old ID.
- Number sequentially from the last entry in the file. Read it first.
- Record the reasoning, not just the outcome. A decision without its reasoning gets undone by
  someone who wasn't there.
- If David made the call, attribute it to David. If it was research-driven, say so and cite the
  source.

A decision recorded here is **not a finding** at audit — that is the point of writing it down.
